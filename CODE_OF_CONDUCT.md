# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not people.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and useful. Personal hostility isn't.
- "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are good contributions.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't.
- Uncertainty stated plainly beats confidence invented — that applies to this
  project's docs as much as its discussions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks, including about someone's experience
  level.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## A note specific to this project

This repository is about software that watches and listens to people. Discussion
of its surveillance implications is on-topic and welcome. Using it to help
someone monitor a person without their knowledge is not, and such requests will
be closed.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, or open a private security advisory if it
involves someone's safety. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
edit, hide, or delete contributions, and may ban accounts, for conduct they judge
inappropriate.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1,
shortened deliberately.
